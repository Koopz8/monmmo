## Things already said more than once — stop saying them

These have each appeared in two or more posts. They are part of the project's
furniture now, and repeating them makes an update read like a brochure:

- The client ships no game data / everything is read from your own file. **Say
  this in #welcome and #rules, not in every post.** The one exception is when a
  post is specifically about new data being read, where one short line is fair.
- "Measured, not guessed." True, and established.
- The test count going up. Fine as one number at the end. Not a headline.
- The general shape of the project — what it is, what it's built on. That lives
  in #welcome and gets synced automatically.
