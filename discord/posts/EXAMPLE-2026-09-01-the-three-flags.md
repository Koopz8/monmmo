Sixteen milestones of work and the walkable world moved by four maps. That is
the finding, not a disappointment — because the walker was never going to say
what is in the way, since **what is in the way is not a wall.**

One hundred and eighty-six people in this game hand something over. Three of
them answer to "standing on ground a player can already reach, hidden behind a
flag," and their flags are consecutive:

```
8.2  LAVENDER TOWN  (3, 3)    POKé FLUTE     flag 0x0035
1.45 ROCKET HIDEOUT (3, 2)    LIFT KEY       flag 0x0036
1.45 ROCKET HIDEOUT (20, 5)   SILPH SCOPE    flag 0x0037
```

That is the whole middle of the game, in three lines, and it was one walk away
the entire time.

**Next:** find what clears 0x0037, then 0x0036, then 0x0035. Each is a script on
a map that is already reachable. Three scripts deep, not three regions deep.
